# Publishing Platforms

## PC Platforms

- **Steam**: Largest PC market, specific requirements
- **Epic Games Store**: Growing platform
- **GOG**: DRM-free option

## Console Platforms

- **PlayStation**: Sony certification process
- **Xbox**: Microsoft requirements
- **Nintendo**: Different hardware constraints

## Mobile Platforms

- **Apple App Store**: iOS guidelines
- **Google Play**: Android requirements
- **Mobile Web**: Browser-based gaming

## Requirements

- **Build Optimization**: Size, performance
- **Content Rating**: ESRB, PEGI, etc.
- **Privacy**: Data protection compliance
- **Testing**: Platform-specific testing

---

**Use this skill**: When publishing games, meeting platform requirements, or distributing across platforms.
