# Particle Systems

## Fundamentals

- **Emitters**: Spawn particles at location
- **Physics**: Gravity, forces, collisions
- **Lifetime**: Birth and death of particles
- **Rendering**: Billboards, meshes, trails

## Common Effects

- **Explosions**: Burst + physics
- **Fire/Smoke**: Layered particles with color gradients
- **Magic**: Colored trails and bursts
- **Environmental**: Rain, snow, dust

## Optimization

- Instancing for batching
- LOD systems for distant particles
- GPU simulation when possible
