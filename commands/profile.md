# /game-profile - Assess Game Development Skills

Evaluate your game development expertise and get personalized recommendations.

## Usage

```
/game-profile
/game-profile programmer
/game-profile designer graphics
/game-profile unity
```

## What It Does

- Assess game dev knowledge
- Get proficiency score (0-100%)
- Identify skill gaps
- Receive learning recommendations
- Compare to role requirements

## Assessment Areas

- **Game Design** - Mechanics, level design, balance
- **Game Programming** - C#, C++, GDScript
- **Graphics** - Shaders, VFX, optimization
- **Audio** - Sound design, music systems
- **Networking** - Multiplayer, netcode
- **Tools & Pipeline** - Automation, asset management
- **Publishing** - Platforms, monetization

## Output

- Overall proficiency score
- Breakdown by skill area
- Strengths identification
- Skill gaps analysis
- Recommended learning resources
- Estimated time to proficiency

**Use this command**: To assess your skills and plan learning!
