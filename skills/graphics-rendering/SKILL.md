# Graphics & Rendering

## 3D Graphics Fundamentals

- **Meshes**: Vertices, triangles, geometry
- **Models**: Importing from Blender, Maya
- **Materials**: Textures, PBR shading
- **Lighting**: Directional, point, spot lights
- **Cameras**: Perspectives, orthographic
- **Rendering Pipeline**: Rasterization, ray tracing

## Shader Programming

**Languages**: HLSL (DirectX), GLSL (OpenGL), ShaderLab (Unity)

- Vertex shaders (geometry transformation)
- Fragment/pixel shaders (color calculation)
- Custom materials and effects
- Normal mapping, parallax mapping
- Procedural textures
- Special effects (distortion, glow)

## Visual Effects (VFX)

- **Particle Systems**: Emitters, physics, forces
- **Explosions**: Timing, scale, variety
- **Magical Effects**: Colors, shapes, animations
- **Environmental**: Smoke, fire, water
- **Post-Processing**: Bloom, motion blur, color grading

## Optimization

- **Batching**: Reducing draw calls
- **LOD Systems**: Level of detail
- **Culling**: Visibility optimization
- **Texture Atlasing**: Memory efficiency
- **GPU Profiling**: Performance analysis
- **Mobile Optimization**: Lower poly counts, simpler shaders

---

**Use this skill**: When creating shaders, optimizing visuals, or implementing effects.
