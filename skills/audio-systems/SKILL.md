# Audio & Sound Systems

## Audio Programming

- **Audio Engines**: Wwise, FMOD, built-in engine audio
- **Sound Playback**: 2D and 3D audio
- **Mixing**: Master volume, tracks, submixes
- **Spatial Audio**: 3D positioning, Doppler
- **Voice Systems**: Recording, playback, lip-sync
- **Audio Events**: Interactive music, dynamic audio

## Sound Design

- **Sound Effects**: Recording, synthesis, libraries
- **Environmental Audio**: Ambient sounds, acoustics
- **UI Sounds**: Feedback, confirmations
- **Music**: Background music, interactive systems
- **Voice Acting**: Recording quality, processing

## Music Integration

- **Interactive Music**: Layers, stems, adaptive
- **Transitions**: Seamless music changes
- **Ducking**: Reducing music during dialogue
- **Dynamic Scores**: Music responding to gameplay
- **Tools**: DAWs (Reaper, FL Studio, Ableton)

## Optimization

- **File Formats**: MP3, OGG, WAV tradeoffs
- **Compression**: Bitrate optimization
- **Streaming**: Large audio file management
- **Memory**: RAM optimization for audio
- **Performance**: CPU usage optimization
