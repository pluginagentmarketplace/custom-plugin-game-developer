# /assess - Knowledge Assessment

Assess your current knowledge level for any development role or specific skill.

## Usage

```
/assess
/assess [role-name]
/assess [skill-name]
```

## What It Does

This command helps you:

1. **Evaluate Knowledge** - Assess your current expertise level
2. **Identify Gaps** - Discover what you need to learn
3. **Track Progress** - Monitor improvement over time
4. **Get Recommendations** - Receive personalized learning suggestions
5. **Benchmark Skills** - Compare against industry standards
6. **Certification Prep** - Prepare for industry certifications

## Assessment Types

Assess your readiness for specific roles:
- Frontend Developer

### By Topic
Assess knowledge in broader areas:
- Web development fundamentals

- Performance optimization

## Assessment Covers

### Knowledge Areas
- **Fundamentals**: Core concepts and foundational knowledge
- **Intermediate**: Practical application and best practices
- **Advanced**: Expert-level understanding and optimization
- **Latest**: Cutting-edge technologies and trends

### Skill Categories
- **Technical Skills**: Coding, tool usage, framework knowledge
- **Soft Skills**: Communication, leadership, collaboration
- **Domain Knowledge**: Industry patterns and best practices
- **Tools & Ecosystem**: Knowing the right tools for the job

## How It Works

1. **Take Assessment** - Answer questions about your knowledge
2. **Get Results** - Receive detailed score breakdown
3. **See Report** - Comprehensive assessment report
4. **Identify Gaps** - Clear areas for improvement
5. **Get Path** - Customized learning path to fill gaps

## Assessment Levels

- **Beginner** (0-25%): Starting to learn, basic understanding
- **Intermediate** (25-50%): Practical experience, can solve problems
- **Proficient** (50-75%): Strong knowledge, can mentor others
- **Expert** (75-100%): Deep expertise, thought leader
- **Master** (90%+): Industry expert level

## Outputs Include

- **Score**: Overall proficiency score (0-100%)
- **Breakdown**: Score by topic and skill
- **Strengths**: Areas where you excel
- **Gaps**: Areas needing improvement
- **Recommendations**: Personalized learning suggestions
- **Resources**: Specific materials to study
- **Timeline**: Estimated time to reach proficiency

## After Assessment

Based on your assessment results:

5. **Timeline** - Understand your learning journey
