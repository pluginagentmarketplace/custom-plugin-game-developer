## Difficulty Levels

### Beginner
- 1-2 weeks to complete
- Fundamental concepts
- Step-by-step guidance
- Clear requirements
- Examples provided

### Intermediate
- 2-4 weeks to complete
- Combines multiple concepts
- Partial guidance
- Some problem-solving required
- Architecture decisions needed

### Advanced
- 4-8 weeks to complete
- Complex requirements
- Minimal guidance
- Design and optimization required
- Production-ready expectations

### Expert
- 8+ weeks to complete
- Open-ended problems
- Real-world challenges
- Performance and scale considerations
- Publishable portfolio piece

## Project Structure

Each project includes:

1. **Overview**: What you'll build
2. **Learning Objectives**: Skills you'll gain
3. **Requirements**: Functional and non-functional
4. **Architecture**: Suggested design and tech stack
5. **Getting Started**: Setup and initial steps
6. **Checkpoints**: Milestones to track progress
7. **Stretch Goals**: Advanced enhancements
8. **Resources**: Links and references
9. **Evaluation Criteria**: How to assess completion
10. **Portfolio Tips**: How to showcase your work

### For Portfolio
1. Select projects that showcase desired skills
