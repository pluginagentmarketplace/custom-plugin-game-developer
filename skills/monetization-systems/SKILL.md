# Monetization Systems

## Models

- **Premium**: One-time purchase
- **Free-to-Play**: Earn through items
- **Subscription**: Recurring revenue
- **Advertising**: Ad-supported gameplay

## Implementation

- **In-App Purchases**: Cosmetics, battle pass, power-ups
- **Economy Design**: Fair and balanced
- **Price Points**: Psychology and testing
- **Analytics**: Revenue and retention tracking

## Retention

- **Battle Pass**: Seasonal progression
- **Events**: Limited-time content
- **Cosmetics**: Visual customization
- **Progression**: Unlock systems

## Tools

- **PlayFab**: Backend as a service
- **brainCloud**: Multiplayer backend
- **Firebase**: Google's solution
- **Custom**: Game engine solutions
